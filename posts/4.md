# Static Site Generation in Next.js

Static Site Generation (SSG) allows you to pre-render pages at build time. This results in faster load times and improved SEO.

SSG is ideal for blog posts, documentation, and marketing pages where the content doesn't change frequently.

This article discusses SSG benefits, use cases, and how it compares to Server-Side Rendering (SSR).
